# E-Commerce Customer & Revenue Insights

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior to identify key drivers of revenue performance and uncover opportunities for sustainable growth.

The analysis uses transaction-level data from Evora Labs, a fictional B2C e-commerce company operating in Australia.

---

## Business Problem

Evora Labs achieved strong revenue performance, generating **$33.97M** with **8.9% growth**. However, only **26.3% of customers made repeat purchases**, while **38.3% had never made a purchase**.

How can Evora Labs improve customer activation and retention while maximizing revenue from high-value customer segments, products, and regions?

---

## Objectives

- Analyze customer purchasing behavior and purchase frequency.
- Measure customer repeat purchase rate.
- Identify high-value customer segments.
- Evaluate product and regional revenue performance.
- Identify key revenue drivers and growth opportunities.
- Generate actionable business recommendations.

---

## Methodology

The analysis follows an end-to-end workflow:

**Dataset → Data Preparation & Validation → Exploratory Data Analysis → Customer Segmentation & Analysis → Power BI Dashboard → Business Recommendations**

### Data Preparation & Validation

- Validated customer, order, sales, and product data.
- Prepared datasets for customer behavior and revenue analysis.
- Integrated customer, order, product, and sales information for analysis.

### Customer Analysis

Analyzed:

- Purchase frequency
- Repeat purchase rate
- Customer distribution by age group
- Revenue contribution by age group

### Revenue Analysis

Analyzed:

- Monthly revenue and growth
- Revenue by product type
- Revenue by state
- Revenue contribution across customer segments

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Customer Retention Is the Largest Growth Opportunity

Only **26.3% of customers made repeat purchases**, while **38.3% had never made a purchase**.

This indicates a significant opportunity to improve customer activation, retention, and repeat purchasing behavior.

### 2. Senior Customers Drive Revenue

Senior customers generated the highest revenue at **$13.4M** and represented the largest customer group with **248 customers**.

This makes senior customers the most valuable segment for targeted retention and engagement strategies.

### 3. Jackets and Queensland Lead Revenue Performance

**Jacket** was the top-performing product, generating **$0.36M** in revenue.

**Queensland** was the leading state, contributing **$4.9M** in revenue.

### 4. Revenue Growth Remains Volatile

Overall revenue reached **$33.97M** with positive growth of **8.9%**, but monthly revenue fluctuated throughout the year.

Revenue peaked at **$4.1M in March**, indicating opportunities to better understand seasonal purchasing patterns and stabilize lower-performing periods.

---

## Business Recommendations

- Implement customer activation campaigns, loyalty programs, and personalized promotions to increase repeat purchases.
- Develop targeted retention strategies for high-value senior customers.
- Prioritize top-performing products through inventory planning and promotional campaigns.
- Strengthen regional marketing strategies in high-performing states such as Queensland.
- Identify seasonal purchasing patterns and optimize promotional activities during lower-performing periods.
